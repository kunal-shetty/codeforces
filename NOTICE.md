## Services the app calls

The app calls these services directly from your phone. Their terms apply to that use.

- **Codeforces API** and problem pages (`codeforces.com`, `mirror.codeforces.com`): problems, contests, your public submissions, and problem statements for templates and AI.
- **LeetCode GraphQL** (`leetcode.com/graphql`): only data that is public on a profile, plus problem statements.
- **Groq API** (`api.groq.com`): only if you add your own key, for AI hints, editorials and the coach.
